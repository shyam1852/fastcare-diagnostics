Frequently Asked Questions

Booking FAQs

Answers to common questions about booking and home sample collection.

How soon will my appointment be confirmed?

Our team typically contacts you shortly after your booking request to confirm the date and time.

Do I need to fast before every blood test?

Not all blood tests require fasting. If your selected test does, our team will let you know before your appointment.

When will I receive my reports?

Report delivery times depend on the test. Most routine tests are available within the committed turnaround time.

Can I cancel or reschedule my appointment?

Yes. Please contact us before your scheduled appointment if you need to cancel or reschedule.

Is home sample collection safe?

Yes. We use sterile, single-use collection materials and follow standard hygiene and safety practices.

Need Help Choosing the Right Test?

If you're unsure which blood test or health package is suitable, contact our team and we'll help you choose.
